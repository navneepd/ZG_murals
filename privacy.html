🔒 Transparency & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When participating in Zubeen's Chords song challenge, providing your email address is entirely optional. We collect only your player nickname and, if provided, your email address alongside game statistics (scores and max streaks).

2. Purpose of Email Collection

Email addresses are collected strictly to notify top winners of the September 19th 1-Year Anniversary prize giveaway. Your email will never be used for marketing, newsletter spam, or shared with third parties.

3. Data Security & Confidentiality

Your data is stored securely using SSL-encrypted database connections via Neon PostgreSQL. Your email is strictly confidential:
🔒 Strict Protection: Public leaderboard API endpoints (GET /api/leaderboard) and live Socket.io events strictly sanitize data to never output or leak player email addresses to the public frontend.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Following the conclusion of the September 19th giveaway and after all prize winners have been contacted, all stored email addresses will be permanently purged from our database records.
